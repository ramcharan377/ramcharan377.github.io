Geometric Knowledge Distillation

Geometric KD — Research Plan

Knowledge distillation compresses large "teacher" networks into smaller, deployable "student" models. My research explores geometric approaches to this problem — leveraging the geometric structure of learned representations (manifold alignment, distance preservation, and topological constraints) to transfer richer knowledge than standard logit-matching or feature-mimicking methods.

This work is motivated by the need to deploy large models on resource-constrained devices like Raspberry Pi and mobile robots, where computational budgets are tight but accuracy requirements remain high. The approach investigates how preserving the geometric relationships between data points in the teacher's representation space leads to better student generalization.
